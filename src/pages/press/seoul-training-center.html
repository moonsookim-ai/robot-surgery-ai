SEOUL, September 15, 2026 — Lumenara Surgical today opened its Seoul Training Center, giving surgeons and operating-room teams across Asia-Pacific access to the company's full robotic surgery training pathway. With centres already operating in Boston and Munich, Lumenara now trains surgical teams on three continents with one shared curriculum.

The pace at which hospitals adopt a robotic system depends on how quickly whole teams become confident with it: surgeons, assistants, nurses and technicians. Lumenara designed its training pathway together with the Arc system so that teams can move from first exposure to independent cases in a structured way.

The Lumenara training pathway

  1. Simulate. A physics-based virtual-reality simulator with more than 60 validated exercises and objective skill scoring.
  2. Practice. Dry and wet labs at the Seoul, Boston and Munich centres, including docking over open incisions and laparoscopic ports.
  3. Proctor. Expert proctors join a team's first cases, in person or through low-latency tele-mentoring.
  4. Improve. Personal dashboards benchmark efficiency, economy of motion and outcomes over time.

“A robot is only as good as the team around it. With Seoul, every Lumenara customer can train close to home on the same pathway, and our proctors can support first cases across time zones.”

Head of Professional Education, Lumenara Surgical

Built in Seoul

The Seoul site is also home to Lumenara's imaging and AI team, which develops the real-time tissue and vessel tracking in the Arc Vision Tower. Placing training next to that team lets clinical feedback from Asia-Pacific surgeons flow directly into the software.

About Lumenara Surgical

Lumenara Surgical designs, builds and supports the Lumenara Arc robotic surgical system and its MicroWrist instruments. Founded in 2016 by surgeons, roboticists and imaging scientists, the company has research, development and training centres in Boston, Munich and Seoul.
