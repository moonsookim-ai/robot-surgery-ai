The platform

One system. Open, laparoscopic and micro‑surgical.

Lumenara Arc combines an immersive surgeon console, a four-arm patient cart and an intelligent vision tower. It docks over an open incision as easily as it docks to ports — and switches between the two in minutes.

Arc Console

Ergonomic, open-architecture console with 4K stereoscopic viewer, haptic hand controllers and eye-tracked camera control. Surgeons stay seated, focused and in full view of the team.

  • Haptic force feedback to 0.05 N
  • Adjustable 1:1 – 10:1 motion scaling
  • Integrated tele-mentoring

Arc Patient Cart

Four slim, collision-aware arms that pivot around a software-defined remote centre of motion — protecting the incision or port site while the instrument tip moves freely inside.

  • Open & MIS docking modes
  • Active collision avoidance
  • Under-5-minute setup

Arc Vision Tower

Real-time 4K 3D imaging with near-infrared fluorescence, energy generator integration and on-device AI that tracks tissue, vessels and instruments frame by frame.

  • ICG fluorescence perfusion view
  • AI tissue & vessel tracking
  • Secure case recording

MicroWrist instruments

A wrist that turns where a hand can't.

Cable-driven, 8 mm MicroWrist instruments articulate beyond the range of the human wrist, giving surgeons true dexterity deep inside the body. Needle drivers, graspers, scissors and advanced bipolar energy — more than 40 instruments, each with chip-verified usage tracking.

Technology

Your intent, minus the tremor.

Every hand movement at the console passes through Lumenara's real-time motion engine. Physiological tremor is filtered out, motion is scaled down, and the instrument tip follows only what the surgeon means to do.

Remote centre of motion

Arms pivot around a software-locked point at the incision or port, so the body wall never takes the strain of instrument movement.

02

Haptic force feedback

Strain sensors at the wrist return tissue and suture tension to the surgeon's hands — knots stay secure, tissue stays intact.

03

AI scene understanding

On-device models segment tissue planes, vessels and instruments in real time, highlighting critical structures before they're touched.

04

Fluorescence imaging

Near-infrared ICG overlays reveal perfusion and anatomy in a single click — confirm blood supply before you close.

Collision-aware arms

A live kinematic model of all four arms prevents clashes and keeps assistants safe at the bedside.

Connected insights

Specialties

Built for the procedures that demand the most.

General & Colorectal

From hernia repair to low anterior resection, wristed instruments and stable traction make intracorporeal anastomoses and deep pelvic dissection more reproducible.

  • Colectomy
  • Low anterior resection
  • Ventral & inguinal hernia
  • Cholecystectomy
  • Bowel anastomosis

Urology

Precise nerve-sparing dissection and watertight urethrovesical anastomosis in the confined pelvis, supported by fluorescence-guided anatomy.

  • Radical prostatectomy
  • Partial nephrectomy
  • Pyeloplasty
  • Cystectomy

Gynecology

Enhanced visualization and articulation for complex benign and oncologic procedures, including deep endometriosis excision.

  • Hysterectomy
  • Myomectomy
  • Sacrocolpopexy
  • Endometriosis resection

Thoracic

Stable, articulated access around the hilum for anatomical lung resections and mediastinal procedures through small incisions.

  • Lobectomy
  • Segmentectomy
  • Thymectomy
  • Esophagectomy

Micro & Reconstructive

Up to 10:1 motion scaling enables supermicrosurgical anastomoses of vessels and lymphatics below 0.8 mm — the frontier of robot-assisted surgery.

  • Lymphovenous anastomosis
  • Free flap reconstruction
  • Nerve repair
  • Vascular microsurgery
