Lumenara details the motion engine that turns a surgeon's intent into 0.1 mm instrument motion

Real-time tremor filtering, adjustable motion scaling and a software-locked pivot point are what set Lumenara Arc apart from port-only robotic systems.

BOSTON, October 14, 2026 — Lumenara Surgical today published technical details of the motion engine at the core of its Lumenara Arc robotic surgical system. The motion engine processes every hand movement at the surgeon console before it reaches the instrument tip, filtering out physiological tremor and scaling motion down so the instrument follows only what the surgeon intends.

Every surgeon's hand carries a small involuntary tremor in the 8 to 12 Hz range. At normal scale it is invisible; when suturing structures smaller than a millimetre, it decides where the needle lands. Lumenara Arc removes that tremor band in real time and lets the surgeon choose how much to scale their movement, from 1:1 to 10:1.

How the motion engine works

  1. Capture. The console samples the surgeon's hand position at high frequency.
  2. Filter. A low-pass filter removes the 8–12 Hz tremor band and keeps deliberate motion.
  3. Scale. Motion is scaled down around the instrument's working point, for example 5:1 for suturing or 10:1 for supermicrosurgery.
  4. Drive. Joint commands are computed for the arm and the MicroWrist while the remote centre of motion stays locked at the incision or port.
  5. Feel. Measured tip forces are returned to the hand controllers as haptic feedback, down to 0.05 N.

“Precision is not one feature, it is a chain. Tremor filtering, scaling, a stable pivot and force feedback each remove a source of error. Together they give the surgeon 0.1 mm control at the tip without changing how they think about the operation.”

Chief Technology Officer, Lumenara Surgical

Why it matters

  • Open and minimally invasive surgery on one system. The software-locked pivot works over an open incision as well as at a laparoscopic port, which port-only systems are not designed for.
  • Micro-scale work. Adjustable scaling up to 10:1 supports anastomoses of vessels and lymphatics below 0.8 mm.
  • Surgeon in control. On-device AI segments tissue planes and vessels as an assistive overlay; it never moves an instrument on its own.
  • Safety by design. A live model of all four arms prevents collisions and keeps the bedside team safe.

About Lumenara Surgical
