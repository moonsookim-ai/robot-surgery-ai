Lumenara Surgical introduces Arc 2, one robotic platform for open, laparoscopic and micro-surgery

Motion scaling up to 10:1 and new 5 mm MicroWrist instruments bring robotic precision to procedures that are still largely performed by hand.

BOSTON, November 3, 2026 — Lumenara Surgical today introduced Arc 2, the second generation of its Lumenara Arc robotic surgical system. Arc 2 is designed as a single platform that docks over an open incision as easily as it docks to laparoscopic ports, and it adds adjustable motion scaling up to 10:1 and a new family of 5 mm MicroWrist instruments for supermicrosurgery.

Most robotic systems today are built for port-based, minimally invasive surgery. Open procedures and reconstructive microsurgery, where surgeons join vessels and lymphatic channels smaller than a millimetre, are still performed largely by hand. Arc 2 is built to bring tremor-free robotic control to those cases while running standard laparoscopic procedures on the same system.

“Surgeons told us the hardest work they do is still done without robotic help, in open cases and in microsurgery. Arc 2 brings the same steady, scaled control to all of it, so a hospital can use one platform across more specialties and more surgeons.”

Chief Executive Officer, Lumenara Surgical

What is new in Arc 2

  • Motion scaling from 1:1 to 10:1, adjustable during the case, for everything from bowel anastomosis to vessels below 0.8 mm.
  • 5 mm MicroWrist instruments with ±90° articulation and 540° roll, joining a library of more than 40 wristed tools.
  • Open and MIS docking modes: the arms pivot around a software-locked remote centre of motion at the incision or the port.
  • Haptic force feedback down to 0.05 N, so surgeons feel tissue and suture tension.
  • AI tissue and vessel tracking in the 4K 3D vision tower, shown as an assistive overlay under the surgeon's control.

“In supermicrosurgery the margin for error is the width of a suture. Scaling my hand motion down ten times and removing tremor changes what is technically possible in the operating room.”

Chief Medical Officer, Lumenara Surgical

Availability

Arc 2 is an investigational device. It is not cleared or approved by the U.S. Food and Drug Administration and is not available for sale in the United States or any other market. Lumenara Surgical will provide information on regulatory submissions and commercial availability as they are confirmed.

About Lumenara Surgical

Lumenara Surgical designs, builds and supports the Lumenara Arc robotic surgical system and its MicroWrist instruments. Founded in 2016 by surgeons, roboticists and imaging scientists, the company has research, development and training centres in Boston, Munich and Seoul.
