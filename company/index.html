Our story

Lumenara Surgical was founded by surgeons, roboticists and imaging scientists with one conviction: technology should extend a surgeon's skill, not replace their judgement. Our teams in Boston, Munich and Seoul design every part of the Arc system — from cable-driven wrists to the motion engine that drives them.
